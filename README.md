# Botium Toys — Internal Security Audit

An internal security audit of Botium Toys, a fictional toy company, completed as part of the Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate.

## Project Overview

Botium Toys is expanding its online business to serve customers in the United States and internationally. This growth has raised concerns about the protection of sensitive information, business continuity, and compliance.

For this activity, I reviewed the company's scope, goals, and risk assessment report and completed a controls and compliance checklist.

## Objectives

- Assess the security controls described in the scenario.
- Identify gaps affecting confidentiality, integrity, and availability.
- Review the compliance practices included in the activity.
- Recommend improvements to the company's security posture.

## Audit Process

1. Reviewed the audit scope, goals, and assets managed by the IT department.
2. Examined the risk assessment and its supporting observations.
3. Compared existing safeguards against the provided control categories.
4. Completed the controls and compliance checklist.
5. Summarized recommendations for the IT manager.

## Key Findings

The assessment identified several areas requiring attention:

- Least privilege and separation of duties have not been implemented.
- Customer credit card information is not encrypted.
- Critical data backups and disaster recovery plans are missing.
- An intrusion detection system has not been deployed.
- The existing password policy is insufficient, and centralized password management is absent.
- Legacy system maintenance lacks a regular schedule and clear intervention procedures.
- Asset inventory and classification need improvement.

Existing safeguards include a firewall, regularly monitored antivirus software, physical locks, CCTV surveillance, and fire detection and prevention systems.

## Recommendations

My recommendations focus on restricting access to sensitive information, implementing encryption, strengthening password security, and improving asset classification.

Additional priorities include establishing backups and disaster recovery procedures, deploying intrusion detection, and formalizing legacy system maintenance.

These measures would help reduce the risks identified in the scenario and strengthen the company's overall security posture.

This activity helped me connect security controls to business risks and distinguish between missing controls and controls that exist but need improvement. It also reinforced the importance of supporting audit findings with evidence from the assessment.

This is an educational exercise based on a fictional organization. The assessment relies on the supplied scenario; no live systems were accessed or tested, and no controls were implemented.

The scenario, checklist template, and control reference materials were provided through the Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate. This repository presents my completed activity and analysis.
